## Agentic Video Games of My Childhood

- **[Kuga Invaders](https://github.com/raux/d3-kuga-invaders)** — An original Space Invaders-style browser game built with JavaScript, D3.js, and SVG. It combines testable game-state simulation with D3-powered rendering, responsive keyboard and mobile controls, anime-inspired enemies, an Overdrive scoring system, synthesized audio, progressive difficulty, offline PWA support, and automated tests.

## Personal AI Tools

- **[KanjiStrokes](https://raux.github.io/kanjiStrokes/)** — An interactive tool for learning kanji stroke order.

- **[PDF Sadoku — AI-assisted PDF Reviewer](https://github.com/raux/zen-pdf-reviewer)** — A calm, local-first workspace for reviewing PDFs and placing constructive notes directly on manuscripts. It supports precise annotations, separate author-facing and private comments, AI-assisted page review and comment refinement through Pi, annotation filtering, and export to Markdown, JSON, or a flattened annotated PDF while keeping documents and credentials within a privacy-conscious local boundary.
